# Chronological Narrative (High-level)

This narrative mirrors the progression implied by the project’s milestone documents and themes.

## 1) Foundations: Domain-Driven Design
Early emphasis on **DDD principles** suggests the system is designed as a set of bounded contexts.

## 2) Platformization: Infrastructure-as-Code
Terraform/IaC work indicates a shift from “idea” to “deployable platform.”

## 3) Reliability: Diagnostics & Operational Maturity
Diagnostics milestones imply ownership of failure modes and traceability.

## 4) Trust: Governance & Refresh
“Refresh” is the real product: dealing with drift, staleness, and correctness over time.
