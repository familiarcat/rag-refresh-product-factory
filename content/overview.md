# Project Overview

## What “RAG Refresh” is
**RAG Refresh** is best understood as a *production-minded blueprint* for building and operating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) as a long-lived system, not a demo.

It combines:
- **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)** patterns for bounded contexts and ownership
- **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)** for repeatable environments and auditability
- **Operational maturity** (diagnostics, failure handling, traceability)
- **Governance signals** (refresh cycles, change impact awareness, trust posture)

## What it is not
- Not a consumer chatbot
- Not a “prompt wrapper” around an LLM
- Not UI-first
- Not a one-off prototype

## 2025 framing (software community)
In 2025, teams that succeed with RAG generally treat it like a **platform capability**:
- Deployable
- Observable
- Governed
- Owned by real teams
- Measured and improved over time

RAG Refresh aligns with that maturity curve and can be positioned as a **RAG Operating System** concept:
> DDD + IaC + Reliability + Governance → durable AI systems
